# Artificial Intelligence

We identify as Homo sapiens (Latin: Homo: man, sapere: wise) because our intelligence and wisdom have enabled us to achieve remarkable feats, setting us apart from other species. For millennia, we have sought to comprehend intelligence and attempted to build intelligent agents. And fortunately, we have made significant progress.

Here are some questions we will try to answer in this book:

- What is intelligence or what does it really mean to be intelligent?
- How can we build intelligent agents?
- When we build intelligent agents, how do we measure their intelligence?

This book will dive into wide ranges of topics such as intelligence, problem solving, searching, planning, logic, knowledge representation, machine learning and more, where I'll try to answer some of these questions to a certain extent.
