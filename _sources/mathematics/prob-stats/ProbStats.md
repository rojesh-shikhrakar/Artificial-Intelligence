# Probability and Statistics

Life is uncertain, it is its nature. Nature is incredibly complex with countless variables and interactions that can lead to wide range of outcomes.Multiple measurements of most physical quantities rarely yield the exact same results. When we already have some data, we apply the descriptive statistical methods to collect, organize, analyze, interpret and present the data. Hence, Statistics is known as the art of making sense of data (and learning from data).

[Descriptive Statistics](./DescriptiveStatistics.ipynb)

On the other hand, scientists attempts to model the stochasic natural phenomena through some mathematical or computational representation to be able make predictions of likelihood of future events and generate more data. However, many parameters for these world models are usually uncertain and unknown to us. Probability frameworks are used to model (describe, quantify and reason about) such uncertainty.

[Probability Theory](./ProbabilityTheory.ipynb)

[Random Variable & Expectations](./RandomVariable.ipynb)

[Probability Distributions](./Distributions.ipynb)

**Statistical information** about the real-world is used to *infer parameters* for a **probabilistic model** which is *representive of the whole data*.
In other words, inferential statistics allows to make estimates about the underlying-nature or process using just a sample of data or when we don't have complete information and to infer the model's parameters that explains the data.

[Statistical Inference](./StatisticalInference.ipynb)

[Bayesian Inference](./BayesianInference.ipynb)

[Parameter Estimation](./ParameterEstimation.ipynb)

These fundamantal concepts are the basis for quantitative research and data analysis. Ideas of hypothesis testing and experiment design enabled scientists to draw valid conclusions from data. Research studies are conducted in order to answer some kind of research question(s) targetting certain population (often too large to examine individually). Hence we try to infer or learn about the population by selecting (sampling) a **representative** subgroup of the population randomly called a "sample".

[Hypothesis Testing](./HypothesisTesting.ipynb)

[Sampling](./Sampling.ipynb)

[Design of Experiment](./ExperimentDesign.ipynb)

Measure of population usually is called parameters, while samples is called statistics.
